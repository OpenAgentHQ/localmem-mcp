# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and respectful to other people
- Assuming good faith, especially across language and timezone barriers
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Taking responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from the experience

Behaviour that isn't acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests,
discussions, and code review — and also when someone is representing the project
in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers by opening a
[private report](https://github.com/OpenAgentHQ/localmem-mcp/security/advisories/new)
or contacting a maintainer directly. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter
will be respected.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and
may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct. Depending on severity,
consequences range from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from
the project.
